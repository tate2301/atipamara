## Overview (max 5,000 chars)

Three companies in Zimbabwe currently run their day-to-day operations on software I built from scratch — a grocery retailer, a scrap-metal recycler, and a mining operation. Different industries, one platform, one engineer who shipped it end-to-end.

If you're a founder or operator who needs the *thing your business actually runs on* — POS, inventory, payroll, reporting, customer analytics, internal tools — and you want one engineer who can scope it, design it, build it, deploy it, and keep it alive, I'm the person you want on the brief.

**What I've shipped recently**

- **Corelith** — multi-tenant ERP. Three businesses run on it today. POS for a retailer, weight-in/weight-out for a scrapyard, asset tracking for a mining operation. Shared HR, payroll, and financial reporting across all three. Offline-first, because Zimbabwean internet is negotiable.
- **CXanalytics (Afrisight)** — designed the brand, built the dashboard, wrote the targeting algorithms behind an enterprise customer-experience analytics product used by brands across Africa.
- **CXAnalytics Query Builder** — Stripe-Sigma-style no-code analytics over MongoDB so non-engineers can answer their own data questions. Aggregation pipelines, schema inference, collaboration, publishing.
- **Afrisight Mobile** — React Native app used daily by a panel of respondents across Africa, with real money in payouts and the connectivity quirks that come with shipping for emerging markets.
- **mediamtx-rtsp-forwarder** — open-source edge-box bridge that pulls Hikvision NVR feeds out from behind a customer's router and into a browser tab inside the ERP. Born from a real client problem — "we want to watch the shop floor from the dashboard" — and useful enough to spin out as its own project.
- **paynow-react** — the de-facto React SDK for Zimbabwe's leading payment gateway. Zero promotion, organically adopted in production fintech.

**Why founders hire me**

- One person owns the whole thing. No design-to-engineering handoff, no ticket queue.
- I've launched, kept alive, and migrated production systems that real money runs through.
- I default to *boring tech* (TypeScript, Postgres, Next.js, React Native) so your team can take over later without a Rosetta Stone.
- I work well in the brief, in the messy middle, and at the "we need this live by Monday" end.

**Best fit for**

- Founders shipping their first internal product (POS, ERP, dashboard, admin tool, customer portal)
- Teams that need an MVP that *also* survives contact with the first 100 real users
- Companies modernising paper / Excel / WhatsApp processes into actual software
- Specialised builds: payments (Paynow, Stripe), MongoDB-heavy analytics, on-prem hardware integrations (CCTV, IoT)

If your project lives in that space, send me the rough idea — I'll tell you within a day whether I'm the right hire and what a first version actually costs in time and money.

The Dynamic Island is a hardware apology so good that people forgot it was one. Apple had to cut a hole in the screen, so they made the hole a place. I rebuilt it to find out why it feels alive.

<Demo id="island" />

The answer is restraint. Every state, from call to alarm to download, is one div with `border-radius: 100px`. Nothing opens. Width and height ride the same spring, so the pill never expands into a panel. It breathes.

The spring matters more than the shape. This build runs a real one, stiff with just a hint of overshoot. Enough to register as physical, not enough to feel playful. Make it bouncier and it turns into a toy. Ease it out and it turns into a widget.

And the text never rides a moving container. Content holds back for a beat after the morph starts, then fades in once the distortion has mostly settled. iOS pulls the same trick every time you launch an app. You have never noticed. That's the point.
